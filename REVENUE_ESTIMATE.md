# 💰 Ad Revenue Estimate for LPG Calculator App

## 📊 Scenario: 1,000 Monthly Active Users

---

## 🧮 Revenue Calculation Formula

```
Monthly Revenue = (Users × Sessions per User × Ads per Session × Fill Rate × eCPM) / 1000
```

---

## 📈 Realistic Scenarios

### **Scenario 1: Conservative Estimate (Most Likely)**
**Assumptions:**
- **Users**: 1,000 per month
- **Sessions per user**: 3-5 per month (calculator apps have lower frequency)
- **Ads per session**: 2 (banner on InputPage + banner on ResultsPage)
- **Fill Rate**: 60-70% (typical for new apps)
- **eCPM**: $0.50 - $1.50 (varies by region)

**Calculation:**
```
Low End:
1,000 users × 3 sessions × 2 ads × 60% fill × $0.50 eCPM / 1000
= 3,600 impressions × 0.6 × $0.50 / 1000
= 1,080 impressions × $0.0005
= $0.54 per month

High End:
1,000 users × 5 sessions × 2 ads × 70% fill × $1.50 eCPM / 1000
= 5,000 impressions × 0.7 × $1.50 / 1000
= 2,450 impressions × $0.0015
= $3.68 per month
```

**Conservative Range: $0.50 - $4.00 per month**

---

## 💡 Key Factors Affecting Revenue

### **1. User Engagement**
- **Low**: 1-2 sessions/month → $1-3/month
- **Medium**: 3-5 sessions/month → $3-8/month
- **High**: 6+ sessions/month → $8-15/month

### **2. Ad Placement Strategy**
- **Bottom banners**: Lower revenue, better UX
- **Interstitials**: Higher revenue, can annoy users
- **Rewarded ads**: Highest engagement, user-initiated

### **3. App Category**
- **Calculator/Utility apps**: Lower eCPM ($0.50-$2.00)
- **Gaming apps**: Higher eCPM ($2.00-$8.00)
- **Your app**: Utility category = lower eCPM

### **4. User Demographics**
- **Age**: 25-45 (calculator users) = medium eCPM
- **Location**: Pakistan/India = lower eCPM
- **Device**: Android vs iOS (iOS typically higher)

### **5. Time of Year**
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Higher eCPM (holiday season)
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: Lower eCPM
- **Variation**: ±20-30%

## 💰 Revenue Optimization Strategies

### **1. Increase Impressions**
- Add interstitial ads (carefully)
- Show ads after user completes calculation
- Frequency: Max 1 interstitial per 5 minutes

### **2. Improve Fill Rate**
- Use AdMob Mediation (multiple ad networks)
- Optimize ad unit sizes
- Test different ad formats

### **3. Target Higher eCPM Markets**
- If possible, focus marketing on US/UK
- But don't ignore your primary market (Pakistan/India)

### **4. Increase User Engagement**
- Add features that bring users back
- Push notifications (if appropriate)
- Save calculation history

### **5. Premium Option**
- Offer ad-free version for $0.99-$2.99
- Can generate more revenue than ads alone
